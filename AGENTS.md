# Coding-agent adapter
Read ENGINEERING.md, .engineering/project.json, .engineering/contract.json and .engineering/state.json.
Read active_work, its journeys_affected and referenced decisions before non-trivial work.
Run ai-engineering check --dir . before and after non-trivial changes.
Respect declared authorities, scope, prohibitions and acceptance policy.
Never edit an approved journey, its test, the contract, .engineering/tool, CI workflows
or CODEOWNERS to make a result pass; propose the change to the owner instead.
Never approve, homologate or retire journeys, or accept work, on the owner's behalf.
Never use the owner's credentials or a token with admin, bypass or workflow permissions.
Stop on material conflict. Evidence is not acceptance.
